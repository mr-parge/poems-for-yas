For Yas

Yas, you light up my days,
In your presence, I’m amazed.
Your strength, your mind, your sweet embrace,
Forever, you’re my favorite place.

Mahal

Mahal, my love, my heart’s delight,
With you, my days are pure and bright.
Forever with you is all I seek,
In your embrace, I find my peace.
